ABOUT THE PROJECT

A calmer first step into open source.

This project turns scattered beginner questions into a guided, practical path. It is for people who are curious about GitHub but do not yet know the vocabulary, workflow, or community norms.

◌

Clarity over cleverness

Useful work matters more than insider knowledge. Each page gives you a concrete next action.

♡

People over metrics

A respectful question and a thoughtful review matter more than a crowded activity graph.

✦

Practice over pressure

Use the sandbox to make mistakes privately before you contribute publicly.
