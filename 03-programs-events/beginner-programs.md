# Beginner Programs and Events

Programs can provide structure, mentorship, or a deadline. Eligibility and dates vary, so use official pages as the source of truth.

## Program types

Look for seasonal contribution events, mentored coding programs, paid fellowships, local meetups, community calls, and project-specific onboarding.

## Preparation

Read eligibility rules early, shortlist projects, study their contribution guides, make a small contribution before applying when appropriate, and track deadlines in your own calendar.

## Avoid scams

Be cautious of programs that ask for money, credentials, or guaranteed outcomes. Confirm organizers through official websites and established project channels.

## Maintenance note
Check official sources before relying on time-sensitive rules, dates, badge criteria, or program details.
