# Open Source Planning Calendar

Use this as a planning template rather than a promise of dates.

## January–March

Choose projects, learn Git, review program announcements, and prepare a short introduction.

## April–June

Mentorship and summer-program activity often increases. Confirm the current application schedule on official sites.

## July–September

Continue contributing, improve your portfolio, and prepare for autumn community events.

## October–December

Some contribution events and end-of-year community activities occur during this period. Verify current rules before participating.
