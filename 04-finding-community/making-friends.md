# Making Friends in Open Source

Relationships grow from repeated, respectful participation rather than from sending many unsolicited messages.

## Start where the work is

Introduce yourself in a project discussion, thank someone for a helpful explanation, or ask a focused question after reading the documentation.

## Find shared interests

Join project-approved Discord, Slack, Matrix, or community-call spaces. Use the same username when comfortable and respect each community’s norms.

## Collaborate

Pair on a small task, review a draft, translate a guide, or share a learning note. Give credit and ask before adding someone as a co-author.

## Maintenance note
Check official sources before relying on time-sensitive rules, dates, badge criteria, or program details.
