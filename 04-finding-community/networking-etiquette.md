# Networking Etiquette

Good networking makes it easier for people to help you and easier for maintainers to protect their time.

## Ask clearly

State what you tried, what happened, the relevant link, and the smallest question that would unblock you.

## Avoid spam

Do not mass-tag people, advertise in unrelated issues, copy the same message into many channels, or demand immediate replies.

## Close the loop

Thank people, summarize what solved the problem, and update the issue when your situation changes.

## Maintenance note
Check official sources before relying on time-sensitive rules, dates, badge criteria, or program details.
